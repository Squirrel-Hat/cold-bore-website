Help test Cold Bore.

We're looking for Android testers for a shot group analysis app for target shooters. Photograph a paper target, line up the corners, tap the holes, and it calculates mean radius, extreme spread, CEP and point-of-aim offset.

What you'd be testing

Every group, saved and tracked.

Each group is saved with the firearm, ammo and distance, so you can track your accuracy over time.

No range trip needed. A printed target, or a sheet of paper with a few pen dots on it, works fine.

The app has no internet permission at all: no accounts, no ads, no analytics.
